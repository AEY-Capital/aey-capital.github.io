Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What this covers

This policy covers "AEY Analytics", a private tool that AEY Capital uses to read the statistics of its own YouTube channel through Google's YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API, and to upload and schedule our own videos on that channel.

Who uses it

Only the members of AEY Capital, signed in with the Google account that manages the AEY Capital channel. The tool is not offered to anyone else.

What data it reads

  • Read-only statistics of our own channel and videos: views, watch time, audience retention, impressions and click-through rate, traffic sources, devices, countries and aggregated audience demographics.
  • Public details of our own videos: titles, publish dates and durations.

The statistics are read with read-only access. A separate upload permission lets the tool upload our own videos to our own channel, set their title, description, thumbnail and publish time, and replace or remove our own uploads. It does not touch anything else, and it does not access data about individual viewers.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to decide how to make our own videos better. It is stored on our own computers and drives. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

AEY Analytics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google account's third-party access page. Stored statistics are deleted on request.
